# OZON FVG imbalance calibration plan

- Snapshot date: 2026-11-12 (Europe/Moscow); intended training window: [2023-11-12, 2025-11-12), final holdout: [2025-11-12, 2026-11-12).
- Available local M30 cache inspected before runs: 2023-10-06 through 2026-10-15; therefore the full reserved final year is not available. No final holdout will be opened or claimed unless a complete source refresh supplies it.
- Source: repository cache `data/candles/OZON_Minutes30.json`, `OZON_Day1.json`, and API/cache H4 for EMA when required. Use completed candles only. Daily lead-in is 12 months per CLI; FVG daily ATR period 14 requires 28 completed daily bars; HTF EMA warms from up to 12 months of daily candles. M30 strategy lookback is max(3+MaxWaitBars, StochPeriod+1); H4 is supplied with the CLI's 12-month lead-in.
- Execution: initial cash RUB 100,000; fraction 1.0; risk-pct 0; commission 0.0005 per side of turnover; lot from instrument metadata; entries execute at next bar open. Stops/targets use daily ATR from actual fill under engine rules.
- Research limits fixed before results: rank by expectancy; candidate must have positive net PnL after commission, PF > 1, and Max DD <= 10% of equity (MaxDrawdownPct in engine; absolute `MaxDrawdown` is RUB). Each training candidate requires >=20 closed trades total and >=10 by entry date in each fixed 12-month block. Final holdout requires >=10 closed trades and same quality limits.
- Stages: stage 1 joint MinGapATR/RequireEntryAboveFVGLower/HTFTrendEMA/StochPeriod with default exits; stage 2 StopATR x TakeProfitATR in both stochastic exit branches for eligible stage-1 candidates; stage 3 local joint refinement and separate sensitivity runs as specified by the skill.
- Budget: stage 1 500 combinations maximum (template is 350 combinations despite the skill prose saying 120); retain at most four eligible candidates; stage 2 up to 2 x 49 combinations per candidate; stage 3 up to 54 joint combinations plus three separate sensitivity grids per chosen branch. One bounded joint rerun is allowed if a sensitivity grid identifies a higher-ranked candidate: center it on that candidate and retain the local ranges. No unbounded retries. Every run uses the same training dates and costs, `-combos 0`.
- Do not update the OZON strategy literal unless a complete, previously unopened final holdout is obtained, one candidate is locked before running it, and it passes the fixed gates.
