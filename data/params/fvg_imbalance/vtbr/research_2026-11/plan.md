# VTBR FVG imbalance calibration plan

Run date: 2026-11-12 MSK. Plan written before the first backtest. Source is the project's cached Tinkoff Invest candle data, refreshed only if needed. Use completed M30 bars and completed daily bars; daily warmup is the CLI's one-year lead-in. FVG geometry uses daily Wilder ATR(14); the daily EMA filter reads completed daily closes (the CLI warms up 250+ trading days). Entry is next M30 open; stop/target use daily ATR at signal, anchored to actual fill. No strategy mechanics changes.

Target windows, subject to exact cached candle/CLI boundary verification: calibration [2023-11-12, 2025-11-12), split into [2023-11-12, 2024-11-12) and [2024-11-12, 2025-11-12); reserved final validation [2025-11-12, 2026-11-12). Use one consistent cached candle snapshot across all comparisons. If the cache lacks the full span, stop before claiming a verified calibration and report the available-data limitation.

Execution fixed for all runs: 100,000 RUB initial cash, fraction=1, risk-pct=0, commission=0.0005 (0.05% turnover), normal instrument lot sizing, next M30 open fills. Calibration metric expectancy. Pre-registered quality gates: positive net PnL after commission, PF > 1, MaxDrawdownPct <= 10%; MaxDrawdownPct is equity peak-to-trough fraction (0.10 = 10%), verified in internal/domain/backtest/metrics.go. A candidate must also have >=20 closed trades total and >=10 closed trades by entry date in each contiguous training year. Open positions do not count. Final year requires >=10 completed trades and same quality gates.

Budget: Stage 1 exactly 120 template combinations plus one independent original-literal control. Retain at most four candidates that pass both year counts and quality gates, prioritizing expectancy, annual consistency, drawdown, and neighboring-grid stability. Stage 2: for each retained candidate, run both provided exit grids independently (all combinations preserved, combos=0); inspect ranked candidates until the grid is exhausted or suitable candidates are established. Stage 3: one local joint grid up to 54 combinations on the selected branch, plus separate single-parameter sensitivity checks and at most one bounded joint rerun if sensitivity requires it. Preserve all grids and reports. Do not inspect/use the reserved year until one complete candidate is locked in writing.
