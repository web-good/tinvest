# MTLR FVG imbalance calibration plan

- Run date / clock anchor: 2026-11-12 Europe/Moscow; CLI computes `now` per run. Use the identical cached completed-candle snapshot for every command and record actual metadata bounds; intended train interval `[2023-11-12, 2025-11-12)` and reserved final interval `[2025-11-12, 2026-11-12)`.
- Source: Tinkoff Invest gRPC candles through `cmd/backtest` and `data/candles` cache. Only completed M30 bars; daily candles completed before the relevant M30 decision. Engine supplies one year daily lead-in (about 250 sessions) for EMA warm-up; ATRPeriod 14 uses 28 daily bars for Wilder seed/smoothing; stochastic warm-up is parameter-dependent and HTF EMA uses completed daily closes.
- Execution: 100,000 RUB initial cash, fraction 1.0, fixed-fraction sizing (`risk-pct=0`), commission 0.0005 per turnover, M30 signal filled at next bar open, same engine stop/target and gap behavior. No mechanic changes.
- Baseline: `baseline.json` from skill reference for research; `original.json` records MTLR's current full literal (core defaults) and is run as a separate control.
- Train: 24 months split at 2024-11-12. Each admitted result needs >=20 closed trades overall, >=10 by entry date in each half-open year block, positive net PnL after commissions, PF >1, MaxDrawdownPct <=10% (engine also reports absolute DD in currency; use percentage for the 10% rule), and rank by expectancy.
- Holdout: reserve `[2025-11-12, 2026-11-12)`; do not inspect before locking one full candidate. Require >=10 closed trades and the same quality limits. Compare candidate and original exactly once.
- Search budget: Stage 1 exact 120-combination template plus original control. Carry at most four admissible, stable entry candidates. Stage 2 at most four candidates x both exit branches x exact template grids, with all nine fields explicit and `-combos 0`. Stage 3 one local joint grid (<=54) on selected branch, plus separate one-field sensitivities and at most one bounded joint rerun if sensitivity warrants. All stages are ranked by expectancy; annual deal checks are computed from full-run trade ledgers by entry date, not inferred from `RecentTrades`.
- If required history or qualifying candidates are unavailable, retain reports and current MTLR literal; do not claim validation or open the holdout early.
