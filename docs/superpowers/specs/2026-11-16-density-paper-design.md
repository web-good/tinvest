# Density forward test

Approved in conversation: a separate simulator consuming live order books and exchange trades, never submitting broker orders. Virtual entries above a confirmed bid density, cancellation on density loss, protective stop below density, take profit at 2R, persistent journal and PnL report.

Implementation decisions (tunable starting hypotheses, not calibrated parameters): RUB 100,000 capital, RUB 10,000 maximum allocation per entry, three simultaneous instruments, entry +1 tick, stop -2 ticks, target 2R, commission 0.05% per side, 250ms order latency, 50% minimum retained density, two-minute entry lifetime, ten-minute position lifetime and five-minute cooldown. All exposed as CLI flags. Existing DENSITY_* environment controls detection. Paper process subscribes to both books and trades for up to ten most liquid eligible instruments (trade subscription limit), independently of Telegram.

Execution uses integer nanoruble prices and the instrument's actual price increment. Passive buy fills require a subsequent exchange trade strictly below the limit; take profit fills require a trade strictly above the target. Touches never fill. Quantity is bounded by the observed trade volume; partial entry cancels the remaining entry and protects that position. Stop/density-loss/time exits sweep a fresh bid snapshot after latency, only for visible quantities. A snapshot is consumed at most once for simulated execution. Repeated trade timestamps are ignored conservatively. This deliberately misses possible fills; it does not reconstruct the exchange queue or guarantee a pessimistic bound on real strategy profit.

Missing, malformed, stale or disconnected market data cancels pending entries, flags held positions as uncertain and requests liquidation when fresh data returns. No invented fills during gaps or shutdown. Reporting distinguishes realized profit, estimated unrealized profit, entry fees on open inventory, cash, reserved cash, pending orders, positions and data completeness. Equity drawdown uses marked equity. Each run is a new uniquely named directory; journal is flushed per event and summary is replaced atomically. Restart does not resume positions; prior run remains intact and marked incomplete if inventory was left open.

Approaches: integrate paper state into existing notification worker (shares lifecycle but couples experiments and production); separate market-data-only executable (chosen by user, isolated lifecycle and no broker order client). Reuse density detector and market data adapter, with a small dedicated runner and deterministic paper engine.
