# TInvest Project

## Overview
Go-based trading/investment application built around the Tinkoff Invest gRPC API. It implements live trading strategies (Golden X, Reversion, Bonds screening), plus a shared scalping core (model/strategy packages) used by live Reversion and the backtest engine. Analyzes the portfolio and sends notifications via Telegram bots.

## Tech Stack
- Go 1.25
- gRPC (Tinkoff Invest API) for market data and orders
- Telegram Bot API for notifications
- Code generation: `protoc-gen-go`, `protoc-gen-go-grpc`, `goverter` (see `Makefile`); `mockery` (v2) for test mocks (see `.mockery.yaml`)
- Build/quality tooling: `mage` (`magefiles/`) drives lint/test/mocks; `golangci-lint` v2 (`.golangci.yml`). See `docs/tooling/mage.md`.

- `internal/service` — business logic, grouped by concern:
  - `trading_strategy/` — `golden_x`, `reversion`, `bonds` (live strategies); `scalping/model/` and `scalping/strategy/` (shared core used by reversion (live) and the backtest engine; scalping live layer removed); `scalping_rsimacd/` — backtest-only 5m long RSI+MACD scalper (`-strategy scalping_rsimacd`, docs: `docs/scalping_rsimacd/strategy.md`). `rsi_ema` — backtest-only 15m long RSI+EMA trend scalper (`-strategy rsi_ema`, docs: `docs/rsi_ema/strategy.md`). `rsi_pullback` — 30m long multi-day RSI pullback strategy: daily-ATR stop/target (у части тикеров — стоп по закрытию бара `StopOnClose` с аварийной биржевой заявкой `CatStopMult`, спека `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-13-pullback-stop-on-close-live-design.md`), two-sided day-state gate, slot-based volume filter, entry block window by buy time (`EntryBlockFrom/To`; the live universe does not buy 11:30–16:00 MSK) (`-strategy rsi_pullback`, docs: `docs/rsi_pullback/strategy.md`; подбор тикеров — cmd/pullscreen, docs/rsi_pullback/screener.md); live-раннер — `rsi_pullback/live` (отдельный счёт и токен, свой файл стейта и тема Telegram, буст доли у тикера, отставшего от IMOEX — LAG_BOOST_*, выключен по умолчанию, docs/rsi_pullback/live.md), сверка живой сборки с бэктестом — cmd/pullparity. `rsi_zone` — 30m long multi-day RSI critical-zone strategy: RSI cross below lower band + close > EMA(N), exit on RSI cross above upper band or daily-ATR stop (`-strategy rsi_zone`, docs: `docs/rsi_zone/strategy.md`; подбор тикеров — cmd/zonescreen, docs/rsi_zone/screener.md); live — гость раннера счёта rsi_pullback (`rsi_zone/live`, общий счёт, владение тикером; docs/rsi_zone/live.md), сверка — `cmd/pullparity -strategy rsi_zone`; per-ticker literals in `rsi_zone/strategy/<ticker>/` (domrf, afks, baza, dias, lent, mdmg, ivat, vsmo, upro, ragr, mtss, aflt, msng, bane, astr, svav, wush, trnfp, ydex, smlt, tatnp, reni, fixr, moex, trmk, unac, svcb, sfin, phor, head, sofl, vtbr), grids in `data/params/rsi_zone/<ticker>/`. `gap_fade` — 30m long intraday fade of a morning gap down: entry on bar `EntryBar` of a weekday when the day opened ≥ `GapATR` daily ATRs below the previous close, target = share of the gap, daily-ATR stop, EOD/next-date exit (`-strategy gap_fade`, docs: `docs/gap_fade/strategy.md`); live — гость раннера счёта rsi_pullback (`gap_fade/live`, общий счёт, окно входа и фильтр дивидендной отсечки; docs/gap_fade/live.md), сверка — `cmd/pullparity -strategy gap_fade`; universe validation with shared params and pooled walk-forward + gate — `cmd/gapwf` (default universe `GAP_FADE_TICKERS`); подбор тикеров по утренней ликвидности — `cmd/gapscreen`, docs/gap_fade/screener.md. `drop_bounce` — backtest-only long on 15m bars with an hourly signal (rewritten 2026-10-13, reworked 2026-10-14, moved to 15m 2026-10-15; earlier versions are in git history): the strategy builds hourly bars from the 15m ones; a signal hour closes above EMA(`EMAPeriod`=200) of hourly closes with the hourly RSI(`RSIPeriod`=6, calibrated over 4/5/6) below `RSIZone` (30) or just crossed it upward staying ≤ `RSICap` (50); the first hour with a green hourly MACD(8,26,5) histogram (`HourMACDFast/Slow/Signal`) at most `HourMACDHours` (20) hours after the latest signal hour arms the entry for `ConfirmBars` (6) 15m bars; it buys at the next 15m open on the first green MACD(12,26,9) histogram on 15m (green at once, else the red→green flip), Mon–Fri only, on Friday only fills before `FridayTo` (11:00 MSK); exits — stop `StopDailyATR` (1.0, calibrated over 0.5/0.7/1.0) daily ATRs (Wilder `DailyATRPeriod`=14 over weekday dailies, frozen at the signal) below the fill, or the hourly RSI(`RSIExitPeriod`=10, calibrated over 8–12) crossing `RSIExit` (70) upward on the bar that completes the hour; the stop never moves (the breakeven, the trailing stop and every off-by-default filter were removed 2026-10-15; they are in git history); a CROSS-only mode (`ZoneEntry` 0); the buy signal carries the stop for risk sizing (`-risk-pct`, calibration runs with `-risk-pct 1`); holds overnight and over weekends (`-strategy drop_bounce -interval Minutes15`, docs: `docs/drop_bounce/strategy.md`); calibration is an in-sample sweep on 30 of 36 months (`-holdout-months 6`), the plateau centre, a stress test without the best trades and a one-time hold-out (skill `drop-bounce-calibrate`), per-ticker literals in `drop_bounce/strategy/<ticker>/` (gazp, ivat, enpg, sber, lkoh, rosn, nvtk, gmkn, tatn, plzl; points from the 30m rules, awaiting recalibration), starter grid `data/params/drop_bounce/grid_example.json`; live-раннера нет, вселенная под него — `DROP_BOUNCE_TICKERS_M15` (`config.DropBounceConfig`), её ведёт скилл (`scripts/live_env.py`), тикер обязан иметь пакет в реестре (`TestDropBounceLiveTickersAreRegistered`); подбор тикеров — `cmd/dropscreen -interval Minutes15` (шортлист `DROP_BOUNCE_CANDIDATES`), docs/drop_bounce/screener.md. `pdl_reclaim` — backtest-only long intraday around yesterday's range, signals on 5m bars before 10:00 MSK and on half-hours built from them after (`SwitchHour`; a position keeps its entry timeframe for the RSI/MACD exits): formations: A on a green bar that dipped below yesterday's low (PDL) and closed back above it, C (off by default) on the first green close above PDH, B on a bullish engulfing anywhere; a formation crossing PDL or PDH upward enters without an RSI gate, a reversal away from the levels needs RSI(6) of the bar before it below `RSIEntryMax` (50) on 5m or, on half-hours, RSI(`HourRSIPeriod`=6) of hourly closes (the hour read up to that half-hour's close) below `HourRSIMax` (30); buys the next 5m open 07:00–18:00 MSK, Mon–Fri; exits — stop `StopDailyATR` (0.5) daily ATRs below the fill, target yesterday's high, RSI(6) ≥ 70, the first red MACD(9,26,8) histogram bar after a green one in the trade, EOD on the last evening-session bar 23:45; re-entry after an exit allowed; per-ticker timeframe switches `Entry5m` (morning 5m entries) and `Entry30m` (half-hour entries after the switch), both on by default and gating entries only; off-by-default filters `GapOnly` (day opened below PDL), `Confirm` (next bar closes above PDL), `TargetMid` (target mid of yesterday's range), `VolumeMult` (the pattern bar's volume above `VolumeMult` × the average of the same-time bars of its timeframe over `VolDays`=20 past weekdays), `BodyATR` (the pattern bar's body and its close past the level both above `BodyATR` daily ATRs), `EntryC` (breakout: first close above PDH on a green bar; also allows any signal closing above PDH; no target, no RSI exit), `LevelsOnly` (only a reversal at PDL or the PDH breakout C), `LevelTolATR` (a reversal's low within that many daily ATRs above a level counts as reaching it) (`-strategy pdl_reclaim -interval Minutes5`, docs: `docs/pdl_reclaim/strategy.md`); per-ticker literals in `pdl_reclaim/strategy/<ticker>/` (gazp — added 2026-10-16 before calibration: core defaults plus the owner's Entry30m 0, EntryC 1, BodyATR 0.1, LevelsOnly 1, LevelTolATR 0.1; SBER, NVTK next), universe `PDL_RECLAIM_TICKERS_M5` (`config.PDLReclaimConfig`). Note: backtest-only strategies `levels`, `momentum`, `smc` were removed after walk-forward validation failed (2026-07-20); `rsi_mid` (RSI 50 cross above EMA) was removed on 2026-10-02 after UGLD 30m calibration lost money even before costs.

## How to Run
1. Copy `env/local.env.example` to `env/local.env` and fill in required values (tokens, etc.).
2. Run: `go run ./cmd/main`.

## Development Notes
- `APP_ENV` selects mode: `dev` runs strategy workers as goroutines (`internal/app/app.go:runDev`); `prod` schedules workers via the `pkg/scheduler` cron-like scheduler.
- Telegram bot integration is used for sending strategy/portfolio notifications.
- Code generation entry points live in `Makefile` (e.g. `make generate`, plus per-service `generate-*-api` targets); deps installed into `./bin` via `make install-deps`.
- Quality checks run through `mage` (from repo root): `./bin/mage tools` installs pinned `golangci-lint`/`mockery` into `./bin`; `./bin/mage ci` runs lint + `go test -race ./...` + mock-drift check — the same gate CI enforces before build/deploy. Regenerate mocks with `./bin/mage mocks` after changing a mocked interface. Note: `go build ./...` fails on the `magefiles` package (no `main`); use `go build ./internal/... ./pkg/... ./cmd/...`. Details: `docs/tooling/mage.md`.
- Golden X strategy settings are centralized in `golden_x/model.Settings` with `DefaultSettings()` constructor. Algorithm knobs are exported fields; fetch-policy constants (`candleLookbackWeeks`, `divergenceFractalK`) remain in-package.
- Notifications go to forum topics of a Telegram supergroup (`TELEGRAM_GROUP_CHAT_ID`, `TELEGRAM_TOPIC_*`); portfolio reports are pulled on demand via bot commands (`/bonds_portfolio`, `/yield`, `/bonds_screener`, `/dividend_screener`); push-дайджест новостей рынка приходит в тему «Новости» раз в час (`TELEGRAM_TOPIC_NEWS`, источник `NEWS_FEED_URL`, дефолт — RSS smart-lab); см. `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-07-15-news-digest-design.md` — see `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-07-13-telegram-topic-routing-design.md`.
- Все записи уровня ERROR дублируются сообщением в тему «General» Telegram-группы (`internal/service/notification/errorlog`, подключается шагом `initErrorLogSink` в `internal/app`). В stdout поток логов не меняется; в Telegram действуют дедуп по тексту сообщения (5 минут), лимит 10 сообщений в минуту и минутная сводка подавленного. Спека: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-11-error-log-telegram-design.md`.
- Буст доли раннера счёта rsi_pullback (`LAG_BOOST_ENABLED/MULT/PCT/DAYS`, выключен по умолчанию): rsi_pullback и rsi_zone входят увеличенной долей, если тикер за N завершённых дней отстал от IMOEX на порог; gap_fade не затрагивается. Спека: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-08-lag-boost-design.md`, механика — `docs/rsi_pullback/live.md` §8.

## Documentation rules
- `docs/rsi_pullback/` (`strategy.md`, `live.md`, `screener.md`) describes **mechanics only**: strategy logic, tunable parameters, the calibration procedure as a procedure, the live runner and its configuration, and how the screener works. It must NOT contain per-ticker calibration write-ups, run results, profit factors, onboarding dates, or per-ticker risk registers.
- Calibrating a new ticker does not mean rewriting these docs. The per-ticker write-up belongs in the doc comment of `internal/service/trading_strategy/rsi_pullback/strategy/<ticker>/` and in the `_comment` fields of `data/params/rsi_pullback/<ticker>/`.
- A measurement on a specific ticker is acceptable in these docs only as a one-line illustration of a mechanism, never as a record of a calibration run.
